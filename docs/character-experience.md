# Character experience

## Find someone quickly

Open **People** and choose Residents, Staff or Crew. A selected person opens a compact dossier:

- **Now:** one purpose, the current blocker if there is one, and three numbered condition bars. Open “Why / what happens next” for the explanation.
- **Life:** personality, people who matter and recent experiences.
- **Record:** custody, charges, time remaining, court date and escape risk; staff show their employment summary.
- **Items:** actual held/stashed contraband, trust credit and phone equipment.

The header's **People** action returns to the roster. **Management options** opens the existing detailed personnel, legal, assignment and economy controls; its compact-profile button returns to the dossier. Details do not all appear on the first page.

## Follow or play

**Follow** watches the person's own simulation. **Play as** takes movement control. Only staff roles have a direct-play entry. Residents and construction crews remain inspectable and followable. Absence, suspension and hard escort assignments can prevent staff entry or take authority back; the camera then follows the response and explains the interruption.

| Action | Mac | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk | WASD or arrows | Left MOVE pad |
| Look | Drag the world with left or right mouse | Right LOOK pad or drag the right side of the world |
| First / third person | V or view button | View button |
| Zoom third person / Follow | Wheel or trackpad pinch | Pinch on the world |
| Interact | E | Interact button |
| Phone | P | Phone button |
| Pause / resume | Space | Pause / Resume button |
| Return to management | Escape or Management | Management button |

The actual main camera goes to the character's eye position in first person. Third person uses a close shoulder boom and completed geometry for camera clearance. A very tight space moves that camera to eye level; the context line explains why. Follow uses a wider overhead view. Leaving restores the prior management view, cutaway, time speed and decision ownership.

Walking uses the explicit game clock at 1×. Pause stops it. Phone/screens, focus loss and cancelled input clear movement; fresh input is required afterward. Existing simulation duties, food, costs and physical service checks continue. Builders retain their own physical construction tasks; inspecting or following them does not complete work remotely.

A [separate prisoner mode is planned](prisoner-mode-plan.md). It is not enabled in the management game.

## Staff duties in person

Use **E / Interact** to open the current duty brief. The work-phone Tasks page explains the actual assignment and any live report. Ordinary services still require real proximity to their duty station or resident. Officer pursuit, intervention, escort authority and radio timing remain authoritative; a phone is not a remote “complete task” or instant-alarm button.

## A phone with a purpose

The character phone is issued staff work equipment. It opens only in direct staff control.

- **Messages:** real pending supported-role decisions, work-call history and remembered experiences. Memories are labelled.
- **Contacts:** actual work contacts and dispatch, with live availability.
- **Tasks:** duty, shift and incident information from the current simulation.
- **Items:** issued equipment; resident possessions remain visible separately in the management dossier.

A work call first shows duration and any terms, then needs an explicit Start. Progress uses game minutes while the phone is open; avatar input is held. Remaining duration survives a save without offline progress or duplicate effects. Incident priority can interrupt it. Dispatch reports current operations; automatic alarms still follow the established physical radio response.

Resident phone-service accounting/read models remain a simulation foundation. The management build does not expose resident direct phone actions, stash retrieval or physical control. Those belong to the future prisoner-mode design.

Native touch feel and device performance require a device run. Editor phone-size checks establish layout and input contracts, not measured iPhone fidelity.
