# Future prisoner mode — planned, not enabled

## Current decision (2026-10-18)

The main game is a prison ownership and management simulation. **Direct first/third person control is staff-only.** Residents and contractors remain inspectable and followable. Management policies, assignments, legal choices and resident stories continue in the management layer.

A prisoner viewpoint may become a separate optional mode later. Do not expose resident movement or interactive personal-phone actions in the current management build. Core and runtime entry gates enforce this; changing the camera must not bypass them.

## Questions for the later design pass

1. Is the prisoner scenario a separate campaign, a challenge inside a copy of a managed facility, or an optional viewpoint with a different progression system?
2. Which goals lead: relationships, routines, rehabilitation, legal progress, survival, or fictional escape drama?
3. How does the player learn rules, boundaries and consequences without turning ordinary prison management into an action game?
4. How do saves, time speed, staffing authority and scenario ownership stay clear when switching experiences?
5. Which possessions and phone choices are essential, and how do actual access, privacy, seizure and monitored communication become readable?

## Prerequisites

- Separate mode/session permission, not a hidden button on every resident card.
- Purposeful routines, believable complete interactions, animation and navigation at character scale.
- Explicit allowed areas, door permissions and escort/custody authority.
- A short authored scenario with a clear goal and ending before a broad open campaign.
- A dedicated tutorial and controls, with measured native touch comfort and performance.
- Separate scenario saves or an explicit reversible copy. A mode switch must not quietly rewrite a player's managed holding.

The resident phone-service/read-model foundation can inform that design, but it does not constitute a playable prisoner mode. Plan and approve the experience before enabling it.
