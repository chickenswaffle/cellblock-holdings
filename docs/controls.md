# Look around and build

In the Unity editor, click inside the **Game** view before using keyboard controls. A new company starts paused so you can explore. Choose **1×** when you want operations and construction to run.

## Mac and desktop

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move the view | Left drag in Select / Inspect, or middle drag in any tool |
| Move with the keyboard | Arrow keys or WASD; hold Shift to move faster |
| Orbit | Right drag, Alt + left drag, or Q / E |
| Tilt | Drag vertically while orbiting, or R / F |
| Zoom | Mac trackpad pinch, mouse wheel or trackpad scroll; zoom follows the pointer |
| Center the facility | Home, or Camera → Center |
| Select / place a point | Click without dragging |
| Pause / resume | Space or the time buttons |
| Discard an unapproved plan | Escape or Discard |
| Camera alternatives | Camera opens pan, orbit, tilt, zoom, 3D / plan, and wall-height controls |

## iPhone and iPad

- Tap to select a component or place a construction point.
- Drag with one finger in Select / Inspect to move the view.
- Use two fingers in any tool to move, spread to zoom in, pinch closed to zoom out, and twist to orbit. A gesture starting over UI belongs to that panel.
- Open **Camera** for tilt, pan arrows, precise zoom steps, 3D / plan, and full-height / cutaway walls.
- Use **Guide** beside the command dock to reopen the guide. On narrow screens use **Settings → Controls** for the camera reference. Time is held while these menus are open.

The compact interface keeps side panels closed until needed. Close a panel to give the facility more space. Phone layout captures are development checks; touch feel, safe areas, and performance still need a run on the actual device.

## First improvement

1. Choose or resume a holding. Explore in **Facility** and select a wall or furnishing to inspect it.
2. Hire a construction crew in the Facility panel. Crew mobilization and daily wages are separate commitments.
3. Open **Build** and choose a category. **Structure** contains walls, curves and doors. **Prefabs** has six units with Shell / Essential / Enhanced furnishing packages. Housing, Hygiene, Dining, Recreation, Administration, and Security contain furnishings. Use **Rotate 90°** before placing furniture; **Dining** also contains the **Meal service** tool. Walls use two points; curves use a start, an end, and a bend point.
4. For a connected starter extension, choose **Prefab wing** and place its lower-left attachment at the starter building's lower-right corner, `(2, -8)`.
5. Check the dimensions and project quote, then approve or discard. Teal is an unapproved plan; ochre is approved construction. Space becomes operational at handover.
6. Start the clock to let hired crews work. Open **Company** to follow cash, unpaid earnings, recurring costs, and the ledger.

Every holding uses a separate save. Home has a prominent **Continue** card and a sorted **Save Library**. Autosave defaults to 30 seconds; change it in **Settings → General**. The header shows saved time or **Retry save**. Closing, suspending, or unfocusing the game holds simulation time; returning does not apply elapsed real time. **Settings → Display** changes interface size, frame cap, shadows and reduced motion; **Controls** changes keyboard/orbit speed and easing; **Audio** controls volume.

## People and compact profiles

1. Open **People**, then Residents / Staff / Crew, or click a person in the facility. **Now** shows one purpose and three numbered condition bars. **Why / what happens next** reveals the explanation.
2. Use **Life** for traits and relationships, **Record** for legal/employment details, and **Items** for possessions. **‹ People** returns to the roster; **Close** restores the facility space.
3. **Follow** watches anyone on site. For assignments, legal choices, personnel actions and activity suggestions, open **Record → Open full file** (or **Management options** on desktop).
4. Choose **1×** to watch individual routines, travel and complete interactions. Staff still need to reach their actual duty station or resident to perform services.

Hired staff arrive by shared transport, park, disembark and walk to duty. Named relationships, fictional gangs/cliques, contraband, conduct and incidents remain part of the management simulation.

## Staff first and third person

**People → Staff → Play as** takes direct movement control. Residents and construction crews have Follow only; a separate prisoner mode is planned.

- **WASD / arrows:** walk. The game must be running; **Space** resumes or pauses.
- **Drag the world:** look. **V** switches eye view / wider shoulder view. Wheel or pinch zooms third person.
- **P:** work phone with Messages, Contacts, Tasks and issued equipment. Calls use game minutes; the phone holds movement.
- **E:** current duty brief. Services still require physical proximity; the phone cannot complete work or trigger an instant alarm.
- **Escape / Management:** restore the prior management view and speed.
- Touch uses separate **MOVE / LOOK** pads, view/phone/interact buttons and world pinch. Resume after a pause, then provide fresh movement input.

First person has full room walls/ceilings. Third person uses cutaway walls and a geometry-checked shoulder camera. Restraints, recovery, arrest escorts, suspension and transport can take movement authority back. [Character guide](character-experience.md).

## Prison Control and visible work

- **Control**: switch facility lights, alarm, gate, lockdown and CCTV power. Alarm starts restrictions and holds building work; restore the gate and movement explicitly after clearing it.
- Hire staff or construction crews, then run the clock. Trips can share seats before departure. Vehicles use reserved marked bays; builders gather supplies, carry them to the work front and use the tools required by the task. Partial builds become usable only at handover. **Control → View Crew** centers the camera on its current task.
- Place CCTV through **Build → Security** or the Control shortcut, rotate its view, then select it in Control for the live feed and coverage guide. Walls, lighting and camera condition affect coverage.
- Shift reports are filed every eight game hours. **Company** includes extra staff and crew wages.
- All seven staff/contractor bays reserved: wait for departing transport or release a crew. Nearby hires can still use a waiting vehicle with free seats.

## Faster building and equipment

- **Z** rotates a new furnishing or prefab 90°. The visible **Rotate 90°** button remains available for touch.
- A single furnishing quote offers **Approve & place another** to retain its type/rotation; every new location is still reviewed and charged once.
- Quotes fold the inspector to leave more of the facility visible. In a prefab quote, **click directly on a proposed or existing wall** where the door should go. The quote updates; **Undo last door** or Command/Ctrl+Z restores it. See [direct door controls](direct-doors.md).
- **Build → Services** contains electrical panels, ventilation/AC and control terminals. **Security** adds lights, barriers, dog kennels, towers and an armory.
- Select installed equipment for its current status and upgrade quote. For a tower/kennel/armory, cycle the officer selector and assign the post. An armory needs an upgrade before armed duty. Training and added daily wages are shown before assignment.
- **Control** includes grid demand/capacity and electricity isolation. Equipment shows why it is unavailable. Power, staff shifts, actual presence and maintenance matter.
