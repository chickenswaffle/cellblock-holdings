# Look around and build

In the Unity editor, click inside the **Game** view before using keyboard controls. A new company starts paused so you can explore. Choose **1×** when you want operations and construction to run.

## Mac and desktop

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move the view | Left drag in Select / Inspect, or middle drag in any tool |
| Move with the keyboard | Arrow keys or WASD; hold Shift to move faster |
| Orbit | Right drag, Alt + left drag, or Q / E |
| Tilt | Drag vertically while orbiting, or R / F |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel or trackpad scroll; zoom follows the pointer |
| Center the facility | Home, or Camera → Center |
| Select / place a point | Click without dragging |
| Pause / resume | Space or the time buttons |
| Discard an unapproved plan | Escape or Discard |
| Camera alternatives | Camera opens pan, orbit, tilt, zoom, 3D / plan, and wall-height controls |

## iPhone and iPad

- Tap to select a component or place a construction point.
- Drag with one finger in Select / Inspect to move the view.
- Use two fingers in any tool to move, pinch to zoom, and twist to orbit.
- Open **Camera** for tilt, pan arrows, precise zoom steps, 3D / plan, and full-height / cutaway walls.
- Use **Controls** to reopen the guide. Time is held while the guide is open.

The compact interface keeps side panels closed until needed. Close a panel to give the facility more space. Phone layout captures are development checks; touch feel, safe areas, and performance still need a run on the actual device.

## First improvement

1. Choose or resume a holding. Explore in **Facility** and select a wall or furnishing to inspect it.
2. Hire a construction crew in the Facility panel. Crew mobilization and daily wages are separate commitments.
3. Open **Build** and choose a category. **Structure** contains walls, curves, doors, and the prefab wing. Housing, Hygiene, Dining, Recreation, and Administration contain furnishings. Use **Rotate 90°** before placing furniture; **Dining** also contains the **Meal service** tool. Walls use two points; curves use a start, an end, and a bend point.
4. For a connected starter extension, choose **Prefab wing** and place its lower-left attachment at the starter building's lower-right corner, `(2, -8)`.
5. Check the dimensions and project quote, then approve or discard. Teal is an unapproved plan; ochre is approved construction. Space becomes operational at handover.
6. Start the clock to let hired crews work. Open **Company** to follow cash, unpaid earnings, recurring costs, and the ledger.

Every company uses a separate save. Closing, suspending, or unfocusing the game holds simulation time; returning does not apply elapsed real time.

## Meet the residents

1. Open **People** and choose a resident from the roster, or click a person in the facility. The camera centers on that person; **Roster ↓** returns to the name list.
2. Read their temperament, four traits, current needs, gang/clique affiliations, and named relationships. Relationship buttons switch directly to the other person's profile. Friendships and conflicts can develop through nearby contact.
3. Choose **1×** to watch individual routines. Residents decide at different times, walk at different speeds, and use separate destination positions. **Home** restores the facility view.
4. Switch the roster to **Staff**. Integrity, pressure, favoritism, and risk explain conduct. **Staff support** costs $2,500 and makes the displayed changes before the next incident check; read its effect and the event history.

The initial social model includes a friendship, a nemesis, and an officer with elevated conduct risk so there are visible stories to inspect. Gangs and cliques are fictional. Staff remain at their stations; patrols and a complete investigation/contraband system are future work.
