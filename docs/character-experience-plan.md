The user wants substantially less information on screen at once, real direct first/third person play as any on-site character, and a phone that connects messages, contacts and tasks to the person’s actual role. Direct movement with an explicit Exit to management and role-specific legal/illicit choices were confirmed. Existing management/building stays available through the same company and clock.

## One character session

- All present residents, staff roles and both crew members use stable IDs. Absence, custody restrictions and hard transport/escort authority explain why someone is unavailable.
- A runtime-only core session owns movement; saves retain legal ordinary positions and loads return movement to AI.
- Input supplies a normalized direction. The explicit minute loop consumes one ordinary-speed step, validates the swept segment and access, and prevents diagonal boosts, pause movement or off-clock catch-up.
- Preserve food, health, wages, construction and physical service checks. Control only suppresses automatic displacement; a cook/worker cannot perform work while standing somewhere else.
- Residents cannot bypass secure escorts or walk freely into staff areas/the vehicle yard. Conservative public-room/yard access derives from completed geometry until editable zones/door permissions are added.
- Guards can physically respond in character mode. An actual radio call holds movement during its established12-minute report; it never instantly triggers lockdown. Hard custody/secure escorts retain authority.
- WASD/arrows and a left thumb control movement; mouse/right thumb looks. UI-origin gestures stay UI-owned until release. Opening the phone, losing focus or pausing clears movement intent.

## Phone: game state and consequences

One app at a time: Messages, Contacts, Today, Wallet, Case and Pocket, adapted to role. Messages reuse the character’s real pending decision cards; contacts reflect actual relationships/family/counsel; Today reads real routine/duty/construction/calendar; Pocket distinguishes held/stashed items. No action sends an external message.

Prisoners need an actual held contraband Phone for a personal handset. A stash is unavailable until carried. Monitored calls use an installed Payphone, service availability, range and sight. Staff/crew have explicitly labeled issued work equipment. A handset is not silently granted to every prisoner.

Calls use bounded saved game-minute sessions, eligibility, quoted prepaid cost, cooldown and once-only completion effects. Extract the existing debit/commission helper so automatic and direct calls share accounting, and suppress automatic calls during/recently after a direct call. Disclose interruption/payment behavior. Staff phone actions must use actual response/report/task systems rather than bypass their timing. The phone blocks avatar input while allowing its call clock and the prison to continue.
